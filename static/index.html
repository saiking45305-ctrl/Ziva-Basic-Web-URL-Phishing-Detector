How to spot phishing links

  • Read the domain from right to left: the real site is the part just before the first single slash.
  • Anything before an @ in a link is ignored by the browser.
  • Be wary of hyphen-stuffed names like brand-verify-login.com.
  • Look-alike letters (punycode / homographs) can fake a trusted brand.
  • Prefer typing the site address yourself over clicking links in unexpected messages.
